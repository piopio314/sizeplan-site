Everything a manual trader needs around the order

RiskCanvas does not predict the market and does not trade on its own. It removes the arithmetic and the guesswork between your idea and the order, and keeps the trade under control afterwards.

Position size from your risk

Choose percent of equity, percent of balance, a cash amount or a fixed lot. The lot is calculated from the stop distance with MetaTrader's own profit calculation, and floored to the symbol's lot step so the risk never exceeds your budget.

A plan you can drag

Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit move together or one by one. Lock the risk/reward ratio or a price, let the plan follow the market price, or type exact values in the panel.

Up to five targets

Split the exit across up to five take-profit levels, each with its own share of the position. The last target is the order's server-side TP.

Review before sending

Every entry shows its order type (market, limit or stop), volume and prices before it is sent. Trading is off until you enable it in the inputs, so a fresh install only plans.

One trade manager for the account

See every open position and pending order in the account, including trades opened on your phone or by hand. Adjust SL, TP and entry, move to break-even, close part or all, cancel orders.

Break-even and trailing stop

Opt-in automation per position: break-even or break-even plus an offset, and trailing by fixed distance, steps, previous candle, confirmed swing or ATR. The automated stop only ever moves in your favour.

From idea to managed trade in three steps

No pop-ups to dismiss and no lot tables to consult. The panel always shows the lot, the risk and the reward in your account currency.

Draw the plan

Click BUY or SELL. A plan appears at the market price; drag the stop and target where your analysis says they belong.

Check the numbers

Pick your risk basis. RiskCanvas sizes the position and shows the loss at SL and the gain at TP in money and points.

Review, confirm, manage

Review the order and confirm it. Then manage it from the Trade Manager, by hand or with break-even and trailing.

Built to be predictable

A trading tool has to behave the same way on every broker, every symbol and every restart. That is where most of RiskCanvas's engineering goes.

  • Works with any broker. Lot limits, tick size, stop levels and filling modes are read from each symbol at runtime. There are no broker-specific presets.
  • Honest risk figures. The volume is never rounded up to reach a minimum. When the broker's maximum lot caps the size, the panel shows the unused budget instead of silently moving your stop.
  • Safe after a restart. A request that was in flight is stored and reconciled with the terminal after a restart or disconnect. It is never resent automatically.
  • Your limits, if you want them. Optional caps for risk per trade, total risk to SL, daily equity loss, open positions and spread.
  • Your settings travel. Save named setups and switch between them in one click. Choosing a setup never sends an order.
  • You stay in charge. RiskCanvas warns and explains; it does not stop you from doing what MetaTrader allows.
